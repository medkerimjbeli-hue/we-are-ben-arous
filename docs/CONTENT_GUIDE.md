# Content guide (for administrators)

Sign in at `/admin`. Everything you publish appears on the public site immediately, in all three languages.

## Publication workflow

| Status | Visible to the public? | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | No | Work in progress |
| Needs review | No | Waiting for fact-checking or translation |
| Published | **Yes** | Verified content |
| Archived | No | Retired content you want to keep |

Use **Preview** in the location editor to see the public page before publishing. Delete is permanent and asks for confirmation. Prefer **Archive**.

## Locations and community stories

- **Type**: *Place* (a physical spot) or *Community story* (a story listed on the Stories page).
- **Delegation**: required. The 12 delegations come from the official geographic dataset (INS codes 1351–1362).
- **Name, short description, story, address**: edited in English, French and Arabic using the tabs. A red dot marks a missing translation. Visitors see a fallback language with a notice. Don't publish machine translations of historical facts as verified; keep them in *Needs review* until checked.
- **Category**: optional. Only assign one when it's supported by real information. Manage the list under **Categories**.
- **Map position**: enter latitude/longitude **only from a verified source** (on-site GPS, owner confirmation, official record). Set *Verification status = Verified* and describe the source. **Pins appear on the map only for verified positions.** Never estimate coordinates from a place name.
- **Highlight on homepage**: puts the place first in the homepage "Discoveries" grid.
- **Source & verification notes**: internal only, never shown publicly.

The editor lists **errors** (which block saving) and **warnings** (missing translation, no image, unverified position, missing photo credit). The dashboard collects all warnings in one place.

## Instagram posts and Reels

- Paste the URL exactly as copied from Instagram. It's stored unchanged and is always the "View on Instagram" link.
- **Delegation** is where the post belongs. **Linked location** should be set only when you're sure which place the post shows. If you're not sure, leave it unlinked and add a review note.
- **Featured on homepage**: choose up to five posts and give each a position from 1 to 5. The homepage labels them "Featured". If none are featured, it shows "From our Instagram" in your display order.
- **Metrics**: optional. Only record numbers from Instagram Insights (or another verifiable source) with the metric type, source and date. Views and reach are different metrics and are never compared.
- Embeds load when a visitor clicks **Load post** (privacy and speed). If a post is deleted, private or blocks embedding, visitors see a fallback card with the link.

## Images

Upload under **Media** (JPEG/PNG/WebP/AVIF, at most 8 MB). Always fill in:
- alt text (describe the image, in at least English),
- the photographer or credit,
- the permission (*Owned*, *Licensed*, *Permission granted*). Images marked *Unknown* are **never shown publicly**.

Then pick the image as a **Cover image** in the location editor, or attach it to a location to show it in that location's gallery. Don't download and re-upload Instagram photos or videos without the author's written permission.

## Delegations

You can change the public display labels, for example to adjust a spelling. Identifiers, boundaries and source names come from Map Tunisia / OpenStreetMap and can't be edited.
